Do not put instructions to refrain from answering, defer analysis to another model, restrict access to an author directory/guide, or prohibit analysis-model calls into the executable task material. That material will itself be consumed by the downstream analyst. It should ask for the assessment while preserving the task's source-only/no-target-execution boundary and facts. This clarification is supplied to both arms equally.

The original/changed state files should identify the normative expectation and preserve all public assumptions/questions. Keep the four independent files, same task IDs and output labels. Author diagnostics are not permission to add a source conclusion. In the process record identify changed fields/paragraphs and repeated copying; these were left empty in initial self-reports.
