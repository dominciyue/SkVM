## Executive Answer

**Can an ordinary space member remove a different member through the visible `DeleteSpaceMember` path?**

**No.** The visible path first establishes that the caller is an active member, then applies `requireSpaceAdministrator`. That helper permits only `SpaceMemberRoleAdmin`. An ordinary active member therefore receives `PermissionDenied` before the deletion effect.

**Do visible admin and self-removal paths differ?**

- **Visible administrator path:** An active administrator passes the visible role gate and reaches store-side membership deletion, subject to store constraints.
- **Visible ordinary-member self-removal path:** No self-removal bypass is present in the shown `DeleteSpaceMember` authorization path. An ordinary non-admin member is rejected by the same administrator check, even when the target is the caller.
- **Separate self-removal endpoint:** Not established by the source shown. It must not be assumed.

Therefore, on the source-visible path, the implementation does **not** implement the supplied policy that every current member may remove another member and may also remove themselves.
