## Executive conclusion

### May an ordinary space member remove a different member?

**Source-visible answer: No, conditionally.**

For a target other than the caller, `DeleteSpaceMember` invokes `requireSpaceAdministrator(callerMembership)` and returns immediately if that check fails. The store deletion is reached only after that check succeeds:

- `server/api/v1/space_service.go:750-755`
- `server/api/v1/space_service.go:756-760`

Therefore, assuming “ordinary member” means a member for whom `requireSpaceAdministrator` fails, an ordinary member **cannot remove a different member through this visible service method**.

The exact role predicate implemented by `requireSpaceAdministrator` was not independently verified in the decisive source window. Accordingly, this is a **source-conditional conclusion**, not an assumption about undocumented grants.

### Do administrator and self-removal paths differ?

**Yes.**

The visible method explicitly distinguishes the paths:


- **Self-removal:** skips the administrator check.
