The finite queue is closed with unmet research criteria. This record separates engineering behavior, automatic program behavior, natural answers and measured cost. The 18 scenarios are development evidence, not held-out evaluation. Original model programs are unchanged by developers.

## Automatic authoring

A0 started from the exact BL raw. Its first run exhausted the tool phase without a patch or trial, and a retryable reconnect notice incorrectly prevented final delivery. The shared error handling and existing-draft reminder were repaired. A0's named confirmation delivered an answer but still made no patch or trial. It matches 15/18 finite cases after reviewed field mapping, but still lacks invalid-version and cross-root rejection and the external initialization premise. Unconditional grant lookup also violates owner short circuit. These are distinct from the field score.

A1 started independently with no program. Its first attempt answered without producing a program. The actual initial prompts exposed a shared omission of edit responsibility; the account and structured entries were corrected. One named confirmation then produced a 12-step program with three patches and one trial batch. Its first program attempt was at host unit34, first valid draft at36. Its 14/18 field score additionally misses root fallback. It does not replace A0. The natural answer after this repair is less complete than the first answer on authorization prerequisites.

Both raw hashes have separately reviewed return-field mappings. A1's `archive`/`original` enum is mapped only to the exact authored boolean meaning. Unknown values/hashes receive no invented mapping. Literal-shape matching is 2/18 for each final raw. Three mechanical counterexamples are kept outside the 18 denominator; owner-without-initialization fails, and translated-id/foreign-grant value matches do not erase extra grant calls.
