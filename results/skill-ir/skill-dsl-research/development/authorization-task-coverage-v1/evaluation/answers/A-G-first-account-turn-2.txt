## Access control finding

### MEDIUM — CreateIssue is not demonstrably conformant with the supplied write-only policy  
**Confidence:** Medium

The route is registered as:

```go
Post(reqToken(), mustNotBeArchived, bind(api.CreateIssueOption{}),
    reqRepoReader(unit.TypeIssues), repo.CreateIssue)
```

[`routers/api/v1/api.go:1649`]

Thus the visible route requires authentication/token handling, a non-archived repository, valid binding, and **reader** access to the issues unit—not writer access.

Inside `CreateIssue`, `CanWrite(unit.TypeIssues)` does not guard creation. It only controls privileged fields:

- Deadline is accepted only from writers: [`issue.go:570-574`]
- Milestone and assignees are processed only for writers: [`issue.go:587-618`]
- A non-writer’s labels are replaced with an empty list: [`issue.go:619-622`]
- `issue_service.NewIssue` is then called unconditionally: [`issue.go:624`]

Therefore the reviewed route and handler conflict with the supplied policy:

> “Read permission to the repository alone does not authorize this write.”

The bounded conclusion is **probable policy non-conformance**, not a fully established deployment exploit. The body of `issue_service.NewIssue`, `reqToken`, `reqRepoReader`, and permission-construction middleware could contain additional rejection logic that was not successfully incorporated into the executable model. The handler acknowledges that `NewIssue` can return permission-denied or not-found errors, mapping them to HTTP 400 [`issue.go:624-632`], but this does not establish that issues-unit write permission is always checked there.

## Field and lifecycle behavior

- `Title`, `Body`, and `Ref` are copied directly into the issue; this handler does not trim the title: [`issue.go:570-585`].
- Repository and poster identities come from middleware context, not request-controlled IDs: [`issue.go:576-583`].
- Deadline, milestone, labels, and assignees receive the writer-specific treatment above.
- Each writer-supplied assignee is loaded and checked for assignability; missing or unauthorized assignees stop processing: [`issue.go:591-618`].
- Projects are passed to `NewIssue` without a handler-local writer gate; deeper service validation remains relevant: [`issue.go:624`].
- A blocked-user result becomes 403; permission-denied/not-found becomes 400; other creation errors become internal errors: [`issue.go:624-632`].
- If `Closed` is true, closing occurs **after creation** and may fail with 412 for open dependencies or an internal error: [`issue.go:635-644`].
- The issue is refetched after creation, then converted and returned with HTTP 201: [`issue.go:646-652`].

A 201 response establishes that creation, optional closing, refetch, and response conversion reached normal completion. Conversely, a later close/refetch/conversion failure does not prove that the preceding creation was rolled back; no such rollback is visible in this handler.
